# Rebolt Kalymnos — Offline Route App

## What it does
Shows you which Kalymnos climbing routes have been rebolted, when, and what work was done — **fully offline** at the crag.

## Setup (do this at home on WiFi)

1. **Open the app** — open `index.html` in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone).
2. **Install to home screen**:
   - **iPhone**: tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
   - **Android**: tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
3. **Tap SYNC** — downloads the full rebolt database from climbkalymnos.com. Takes a few seconds.
4. Done! The app and data are now cached offline.

## On the island (no data needed)
- Open the app from your home screen
- Search by route name, sector, or bolter
- Filter by: All / Fully Rebolted / Partial / Anchor Only / Year
- The data stays available without any internet connection

## Keeping it fresh
When you're back on WiFi (e.g., at your apartment), tap SYNC again to grab the latest updates.

## Data source
Data comes from the Rebolt Kalymnos maintenance log at climbkalymnos.com, maintained by Aris Theodoropoulos and the Rebolt Kalymnos nonprofit.

## Hosting tip (optional)
For the easiest phone access, you can host these files for free on GitHub Pages or Netlify, then just open the URL on your phone. Or transfer the folder to your phone and open index.html directly.
